FINAL PROJECT / 06 FALL 2025
FINAL BUILD

ASL Alexa.

A gesture-controlled smart home designed with Deaf and hard-of-hearing accessibility in mind. Our camera and MediaPipe recognize ASL-inspired hand gestures, MQTT relays the commands, and a live dashboard and fully rendered Three.js room visualize changes to lights, blinds, temperature, and doors.

My role: Product development, accessibility framing, gesture vocabulary, interaction flows, design tradeoffs, and final presentation.
