01 / 3D PRODUCT CONCEPTINTERACTIVE PROTOTYPE

BrewBar.

I wanted one machine that could make pretty much anything I'm in the mood for. Coffee in the morning, matcha in the afternoon, maybe a mocktail at night. So I made BrewBar, a 3D concept with a touchscreen, built-in ice, mugs, coasters, and even a Spotify feature.
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom · Choose a drink · Simulate brewingOPEN FULL SCREEN ↗
01 / INTERFACEPick your drink

Choose coffee, tea, a mocktail, or a cocktail, then customize it how you like.

02 / EXPERIENCEMusic while you make it

I added a Spotify-inspired feature because making a drink is better with music.

03 / OBJECT DESIGNThe little details

There's a spot for mugs, glasses, and coasters, plus built-in ice. Less clutter on the counter.

04 / PROTOTYPINGPlay around with it

Spin the machine around, choose something to drink, and watch it 'make' your order.

Just a concept for now. The drink-making and Spotify features are simulated, and the cocktail menu is intended for adults of legal drinking age.

02 / 3D PRODUCT CONCEPTINTERACTIVE PROTOTYPE

MirrorMix.

I always feel like my outfit needs the right music to go with it. So I made a mirror that could look at your outfit and makeup, figure out the vibe, and put together a Spotify playlist to match. Basically, I wanted getting ready to have its own soundtrack.
Rotate the 3D mirror · Explore the vibe scan · Generate a playlistOPEN FULL SCREEN ↗
01 / PERSONAL STYLEIt starts with your look

The idea is that the mirror picks up on your outfit, makeup, and overall style.

02 / MUSICThen comes the playlist

Something soft and dreamy? Or more of a going-out look? The music changes with the vibe.

03 / INTERACTIONSee what it picks up

The screen shows how the mirror reads your look and what music it would choose.

04 / PROTOTYPINGTry the prototype

You can explore the 3D mirror and try out the pretend scan and playlist generator.

This is a concept demo, so the outfit scan and Spotify features aren't actually connected yet. They're simulated to show how the idea could work.
