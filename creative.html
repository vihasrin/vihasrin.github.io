01 / 3D PRODUCT CONCEPTINTERACTIVE PROTOTYPE

BrewBar.

What if your countertop could be your favorite café, tea bar, and cocktail lounge? BrewBar imagines an all-in-one beverage station with an integrated touchscreen, a music-inspired experience, built-in mug and coaster storage, ice, and a menu that moves from espresso to matcha to zero-proof drinks and cocktails.
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom · Choose a drink · Simulate brewingOPEN FULL SCREEN ↗
01 / INTERFACEOne touchscreen, endless drinks

Coffee, tea, mocktails, and cocktails with size, milk, ice, and recipe options.

02 / EXPERIENCEMusic meets ritual

A conceptual Spotify connection pairs playlists with the drink you're making.

03 / OBJECT DESIGNEverything in its place

Integrated mugs, glassware, coasters, a drink dock, and an imagined ice system.

04 / PROTOTYPINGTry it in 3D

Rotate the machine, explore its screen, and watch simulated dispensing and ice.

BrewBar is an exploratory concept, not a manufactured product. Dispensing, ice, and Spotify connectivity are simulated in the prototype. Cocktail options are intended for adults of legal drinking age.

02 / 3D PRODUCT CONCEPTINTERACTIVE PROTOTYPE

MirrorMix.

A mirror that gets your look and sets the soundtrack. MirrorMix imagines a smart mirror that reads the colors, style, and energy of your outfit and makeup, translates that aesthetic into a musical mood, and creates a Spotify-inspired playlist to match. Your getting-ready ritual, with a soundtrack as intentional as your look.
Rotate the 3D mirror · Explore the vibe scan · Generate a playlistOPEN FULL SCREEN ↗
01 / PERSONAL STYLEYour outfit sets the mood

An imagined camera analyzes outfit colors, styling, and makeup to interpret your aesthetic.

02 / MUSICSoundtrack your vibe

A playlist concept maps the look to a musical mood, tempo, and genre.

03 / INTERACTIONA mirror with a point of view

On-mirror visual feedback makes the getting-ready experience feel personal and playful.

04 / PROTOTYPINGExplore it in 3D

Interact with the rendered mirror and its simulated scan-to-playlist flow.

MirrorMix is a speculative 3D concept. The camera scan, style analysis, Spotify saving, and playlist tracks are simulated; no real outfit or makeup recognition or Spotify connection is implemented in this demo.
